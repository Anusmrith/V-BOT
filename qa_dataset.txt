User: How does a transformer model generate text?
Assistant: A transformer generates text autoregressively through next-token prediction. At each step, it calculates probability distributions over the vocabulary based on previous tokens, samples the next token, and appends it to the context.

User: What is causal self-attention?
Assistant: Causal self-attention is a mechanism in decoder-only transformers that allows each token in a sequence to attend to previous tokens while masking future tokens using a lower-triangular matrix.

User: What is overfitting?
